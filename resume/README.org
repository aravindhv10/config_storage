* Research Topics

** MSc
Investigated validity bounds of the classical linear superposition principle in multi-slit quantum optical and wave interference experiments:
- Analyzed non-classical (looped) wave trajectories in triple-slit geometries arising from exact boundary conditions in Maxwell and Helmholtz equations.
- Quantified the non-zero Sorkin parameter ($\epsilon$) in wave mechanics setups and established theoretical limits for testing foundational quantum superposition.

** PhD
My PhD research focused on phenomenological particle physics at the LHC, combining theoretical Beyond the Standard Model (BSM) physics with deep learning, multivariate analysis, and high-performance computing techniques:
- *Unsupervised Anomaly Detection*: Formulated model-agnostic autoencoder architectures
    to isolate non-Standard Model events in high-dimensional kinematic phase space
    without assuming specific signal hypotheses.
- *Boosted Jet Tagging & Machine Learning*: Applied deep neural network topologies
    (CNNs, Autoencoders) to classify boosted top-quark jet substructures and suppress heavy QCD backgrounds.
- *Top Quark Polarization & Spin Observables*: Formulated angular and kinematic spin-sensitive
    observables for boosted top quarks at HL-LHC energies to probe chiral couplings
    in exotic sector models.
- *BSM Higgs Boson Searches*: Conducted collider phenomenology, decay analysis,
    and background optimization for heavy charged Higgs $ \left( H^{\pm} \rightarrow t b \right) $
    and light pseudoscalar Higgs $\left( A^{0} \right)$ states in 2HDM and NMSSM frameworks.
